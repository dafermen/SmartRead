# Original plan comparison

The two original project-plan drafts described the same product direction with minor
differences in wording. One emphasized mentoring and teaching structure; the other
emphasized product documentation.

Shared MVP scope:

- Read selected text and full-page content.
- Play, pause, resume, and stop.
- Previous and next sentence.
- Voice, language, speed, and volume.
- Local settings persistence.

Both plans also listed possible future areas such as accounts, payments, translation,
summaries, MP3 export, premium features, and cloud synchronization. Those ideas are
not part of the current local-first MVP unless explicitly approved in a future phase.
